# faq
19
What is Notelink?
It's a notepad that calculates. You write each expense on its own line, the answer shows up beside it, and a “Total” line adds up everything above.
20
What happens to the links I copy?
They're saved in the pinned Clipboard tab, with a thumbnail, title and domain. If the link is a product with a price, we suggest it as a possible expense and you decide whether it goes into the note.
21
How much does it cost?
Basic costs $0.99 and includes up to 7 lines and 3 links. Pro costs $1.99 and has no limits.
22
How do I share a line?
Press and hold the line: it comes into focus and a menu appears with Share, Copy line, Copy result and Delete.
