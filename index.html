# faq
26
What is Notelink?
It's a notepad that calculates. You write each expense or income on its own line, the answer shows up beside it, and a “Total” line adds up the block while the footer keeps spent, income and balance in view.
27
What happens to the links I copy?
They're saved automatically in the pinned Clipboard tab, grouped by date and category, with a thumbnail, title and price when there is one. If it's a product with a price, we suggest it as a possible expense.
28
How much does it cost?
Basic costs $0.99 and includes up to 7 lines, 3 links and 2 tabs. Pro costs $1.99 and removes every limit, with unlimited tabs and the Cards view.
29
How do I share a line?
Press and hold the line: it comes into focus and a menu appears with Share, Copy line, Copy result, Statistics and Delete.
30
Can I plan bills and back up my notes?
Yes. The calendar holds planned expenses, income, monthly subscriptions and reminders two days before, and you can export or import all your notes, links, debts and settings as a JSON backup.
